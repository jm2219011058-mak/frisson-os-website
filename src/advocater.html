We hold this conviction: every leap in how life is experienced begins with the relentless imagination of different possibilities.

Human life is a complex system woven from biology, memory, emotion, environment, relationship, and inner meaning — far richer than any single dimension could capture. It is our respect for this complexity that gives the world Frisson creates its purpose.

We are the people shaping the future of living. With that power already in hand, the question is no longer “can we create” — but “what do we choose to create.”

Founding Catalyst

A circle of creators shaping the future of living.

Frisson continues to attract people from architecture, art, cinematic world building, bioengineering, sensory research, and frontier technology, because the boundaries they are each exploring are converging around a shared ambition: Rebuild Respect for the Human.

Film and art shape emotion, memory, and a sense of world; architecture translates imagination into inhabitable reality; biology and sensory research begin with the body and the mechanisms of life, examining how light, sound, air, materials, and rhythm influence perception, emotion, and long-term states of being.

This circle brings together world builders who have shaped global film, animation, and immersive environments; artists whose work has entered international cultural contexts across New York, Paris, Milan, Florence, and Lisbon; cross-disciplinary practitioners working across biomedical engineering, human perception, sound, and spatial storytelling; and creators with backgrounds in synthetic biology, life sciences, and frontier technology. It also carries experience connected to living networks reaching 1.65 billion annual visits and more than $10 billion in scale.

Frisson’s pull lies in bringing these once-separate capabilities into a shared system of living: allowing worldbuilding to enter reality, perception to become spatial language, biology to inform design, and technology to respond more sensitively to the body and the rhythms of life — ultimately shaping a future of living that holds greater respect for human complexity, sensitivity, and lived experience.
